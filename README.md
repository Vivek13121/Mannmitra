# MannMitra

**Digital Mental Health & Psychological Support Platform for Students**

MannMitra is a digital mental health platform designed to make psychological support more accessible and proactive for college students. It combines **emotional analysis, personalized support, counsellor access, crisis support, and institutional insights** in one platform.

## Key Features

### Emotional & Risk Analysis

Students can share their experiences through **text and voice inputs**, which are analyzed to identify emotional and stress-related signals and provide appropriate support.

### Personalized AI Support

The platform provides personalized wellness guidance and first-aid support based on the user's needs, while maintaining a clear distinction from professional counselling.

### Crisis Support

MannMitra includes a crisis-support flow for situations indicating severe distress, helping users quickly access appropriate emergency and professional support resources.

### Counsellor Connect

Students can discover counsellors based on factors such as specialization, language, availability, and session mode, and book confidential appointments directly through the platform.

### Institutional Insights

An admin dashboard provides **anonymous emotion trends and high-risk alerts**, helping institutions identify patterns in student well-being and periods where additional support may be needed.

The dashboard can be used to compare patterns across periods such as regular days, assignment deadlines, examination weeks, and holidays.

## How It Works

```text
Student
   |
   +-- Text / Voice Input
   |
   v
Emotion & Risk Analysis
   |
   +-- Personalized Support
   |
   +-- Counsellor Connect
   |
   +-- Crisis Support
            |
            v
     Institutional Insights
            |
            v
       Admin Dashboard
```

The platform connects **individual-level support** with **institution-level insights**, allowing students to receive support while helping institutions better understand broader well-being patterns.

## Privacy & Responsible Use

MannMitra is designed with privacy as an important part of the platform. Institutional insights focus on **anonymous trends** rather than unnecessary exposure of individual student information.

The platform is intended to provide **support and early intervention**, not to replace qualified mental health professionals, clinical diagnosis, or emergency services.
